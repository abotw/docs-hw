## 📦 2) Unboxing & Setup

Start with the **hardware setup** before using your printer:

### 👇 Step-by-Step

1.  **Unpack the Printer**
    Remove all packaging, tapes, and protective materials. Place the printer on a stable surface.
2.  **Connect Power & Turn On**
    Plug the power cable into the printer and wall outlet, then press the power button.
3.  **Fill the Ink Tanks**
    The Smart Tank has visible refillable ink tanks — fill them with the provided HP ink bottles. (Avoid overfilling.)
4.  **Install Printheads / Remove Protective Clips**
    Follow the instructions on the setup guide to install the printheads and remove all protective parts.
5.  **Load Paper**
    Put plain A4 paper into the input tray.
6.  **Printhead Alignment**
    After powering on, the printer will prompt for alignment — this ensures accurate print quality.

These steps are similar to the wider Smart Tank series; HP provides **detailed setup guides** on its official support site. ([support.hp.com](https://support.hp.com/tw-zh/document/ish_4505660-4317267-16?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

## 🖨️ 4) Basic Use

### ✏️ Printing

-   Open a document, photo, or web page, and choose **Print**.
-   Select the HP Smart Tank 672 as your printer.
-   Adjust settings (color, quality, paper size) before printing.

### 📄 Scanning

-   Place your document face-down on the scanner glass.
-   Use the **HP Smart app** or your system’s scanning tool.
-   Save scans as JPEG or PDF.

### 📚 Copying

-   Place the original on the scanner glass.
-   Press **Copy** on the printer UI or via the HP Smart app.
